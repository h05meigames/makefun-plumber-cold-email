# The Plumber's Inbox: A 120-Word Cold Email

Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Plumber's Inbox: A 120-Word Cold Email"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ravendell-the-plumber-s-inbox-a-120-word-cold-email-9ab3e51c) — one cold email of about 120 words, subject line included, from a bookkeeping service to a busy plumber.

Join make.fun with my referral: https://make.fun/r/0ene8b3q

## The email

> Subject: Your books, done before your van leaves at 6
>
> Hi,
>
> I know your day starts before 6, the van is loaded by 7, and the paperwork waits until the kids are in bed. That is the bit I take off your plate.
>
> I am Sarah from Ledgerline Bookkeeping. We keep the books for 14 local trades: invoices chased, receipts sorted, VAT filed, so nothing lands on your kitchen table at 10pm.
>
> First step is small. Send me last month's invoices and I will reconcile them for £45, flat. No contract, no monthly fee until you say so.
>
> If it helps, we keep going. If not, the books are tidy and you are free.
>
> Could I look at October's invoices this week?
>
> Sarah

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the email to `/output/entry.txt` — UTF-8, no trailing newline, 123 words including the subject line (enforced by a word-count guard: 100–140 words).

```sh
node main.ts
```
